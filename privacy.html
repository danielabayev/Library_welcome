Privacy Policy

My Library App (package com.mylibrary.app) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

My Library App is a personal and household book library. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Daniel Abayev ("we"). Contact: mylibraryappmail@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Shelf photos you choose to share

This is optional and nothing happens without your consent. If you edit or skip at least 10% of the books you review from a scan, the app may ask whether we can keep that photo. It shows you the photo and asks every time. Saying no changes nothing in the app.

What we keepThe photo; what the scan detected for each book (title, author, year, ISBN); whether you kept, edited, skipped or did not review it; your corrected title, author, year and ISBN; the name of the model used; the version of the consent text; and the date. It is linked to your account so that you can delete it. Your reading status, ratings, notes and groups are not included. The app re-saves the photo before sending it, and our server removes any remaining photo metadata such as location and camera details.
WhyOnly to measure how well the scan works and to compare AI models, so we can find one that reads shelves better. We do not use shared photos to train AI models, for advertising, or for any other purpose. If that ever changes, we will ask you again first.
Who can see itThe people who maintain the app may view it. To compare models we may send it to AI model providers, who process it under their own terms. Other users of the app cannot see it. We do not sell it.
SecurityIt is kept in private storage that app users cannot read directly. Access goes through our server, and it is encrypted in transit.
LimitsAt most 3 shared photos per user at a time. Each is deleted automatically 12 months after you share it; a daily job does the removal.
Your controlDelete all your shared photos at any time in Settings → Delete my shared photos, which withdraws your consent for them. You can turn the question off there or with "Don't ask again". Deleting your account also deletes them. You can also email us.

For users in the EEA and UK, our legal basis for keeping a shared photo is your consent. Withdrawing it does not affect processing done before. Please share only photos that show books, with no people, names, addresses or documents.

Permissions

  • Camera - to scan barcodes and take shelf photos. Optional: you can pick photos from the gallery or add books by hand.
  • Notifications - to show your reading reminder. Optional.
  • Internet - to sync your library and look up books.
  • Receive boot completed - to restore your reminder after the phone restarts.

Who receives data (service providers)

  • Supabase - hosts our database, sign-in and server functions. Your account and library content are stored there.
  • Google - Sign in with Google; the Gemini API (shelf photo recognition); the Google Books API (book details). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive) - fallback source of book details and covers, receiving only ISBNs or search terms.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each library is restricted by database rules: you can read your own data and your household's; people you share with can only read your library, request to borrow, and claim wishlist gifts.

Sharing with other people

What you share is your choice. When you share your library with someone by email, they can see your books, including the status, rating and notes shown on a book. Household members can view and edit the shared household library. You can stop sharing at any time.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists, except shared shelf photos, which are deleted after 12 months at the latest. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, books, groups, wishlist, shares, borrow requests, gift claims and any shelf photos you shared from our servers. A household you created is deleted for its members as well. You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account. Copies may remain for a limited time in the backups and logs of our service providers.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

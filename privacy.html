Privacy Policy

My Library App (package com.mylibrary.app) · Last updated: 26 November 2026

My Library App is a personal and household book library. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who receives it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Daniel Abayev ("we"). Contact: mylibraryappmail@gmail.com.

Information we collect

InformationWhy
Account details - your Google account email, display name and a user id, received when you sign in with Google.To sign you in, show who you are to people you share with, and let you find friends by email.
Library content - books (title, author, year, ISBN, cover link, reading status, rating, personal notes, who borrowed it), groups of books, and your wishlist.This is the core function of the app. It is stored in your account so it syncs between devices.
Sharing and social data - households you create or join, people you share your library with, borrow requests, and gift claims on wishlist items.To let family and friends see your library, ask to borrow books and coordinate gifts. The owner of a wishlist item never sees who claimed it, so gifts stay a surprise.
Camera images - barcode scans, and shelf photos you choose to scan.Barcodes are read on your device. A shelf photo is sent once to our server and on to Google's Gemini service to recognise the books on it. We do not store the photo. Google handles it under the Gemini API terms: it may keep it for a limited time, and depending on the service tier may use it to improve its products, including with human review. Photograph only your books, with no people or personal documents.
Search terms and ISBNs you look up.Sent to our server and on to Google Books and Open Library to fetch book details and covers.
Reading reminder settings and your chosen theme.Stored only on your device. Reminders are local notifications; no data is sent anywhere.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising id, or device identifiers for tracking.

Permissions

  • Camera - to scan barcodes and take shelf photos. Optional: you can pick photos from the gallery or add books by hand.
  • Notifications - to show your reading reminder. Optional.
  • Internet - to sync your library and look up books.
  • Receive boot completed - to restore your reminder after the phone restarts.

Who receives data (service providers)

  • Supabase - hosts our database, sign-in and server functions. Your account and library content are stored there.
  • Google - Sign in with Google; the Gemini API (shelf photo recognition); the Google Books API (book details). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy.
  • Open Library (Internet Archive) - fallback source of book details and covers, receiving only ISBNs or search terms.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access to each library is restricted by database rules: you can read your own data and your household's; people you share with can only read your library, request to borrow, and claim wishlist gifts.

Sharing with other people

What you share is your choice. When you share your library with someone by email, they can see your books, including the status, rating and notes shown on a book. Household members can view and edit the shared household library. You can stop sharing at any time.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Delete my account. This permanently removes your profile, books, groups, wishlist, shares, borrow requests and gift claims from our servers. A household you created is deleted for its members as well. You can also request deletion by email; see how to delete your account. Copies may remain for a limited time in the backups and logs of our service providers.

Your rights

You may ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA) you may have additional rights, which we will honour.

Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date above.

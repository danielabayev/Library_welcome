Delete your My Library App account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Library App and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

By email

If you can no longer open the app, email mylibraryappmail@gmail.com from the address of your Google account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

Profile, books and notes, book groups, wishlist, library shares, borrow requests and gift claims. A household you created is deleted for its members too. Copies may remain for a limited time in the backups and logs of our service providers.
